How It Works

Three steps to emotionally-aware conversations

1

Send a Message

Type or speak in any language. EmoBot supports multilingual input via text and voice.

2

Emotion Detection

A fine-tuned XLM-RoBERTa model analyzes your text and identifies one of 28 distinct emotions.

3

Empathetic Response

Gemini AI crafts a reply that naturally aligns with your emotional state and language.

Packed With Features

Everything you need for meaningful AI conversations

28 Emotions

Fine-tuned XLM-RoBERTa on the GoEmotions dataset — from joy and love to grief and anger.

Multilingual

Understands and responds in English, French, Arabic, Spanish, and many more languages.

Voice Input

Speak to EmoBot using Whisper or your browser's built-in speech recognition.

Text-to-Speech

Listen to responses with natural Edge TTS voices in multiple languages.

Export as JSON

Download your entire conversation history for analysis or model training.

Feedback Loop

Like or dislike responses to help improve the AI for future training iterations.
